# Micophone Privacy Policy

Micophone turns your Android phone into a microphone for your own computer.

**Microphone audio.** While you have streaming turned on (shown by a notification), Micophone
records from the phone's microphone and sends the audio directly to the computer you chose,
over your local Wi-Fi network or a USB cable. The audio is not sent to the developer or to any
other server, and it is not stored on the phone.

**Network.** Micophone only talks to computers on your local network or USB connection that run
the Micophone PC app. To find them, it sends a broadcast message on your local network. It does
not use the internet. The only exception is the optional *Install USB support* button in the PC app,
which, when you click it, downloads Google's Android platform-tools from dl.google.com.

**Data we collect.** None. Micophone has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.
It stores your settings (PC address, port, options) on your phone only.

**Permissions.** *Microphone*: to capture your voice. *Notifications*: to show that the
microphone is on and give you Mute/Stop controls. *Network access*: to reach your PC.
